# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Primary: recruiters and hiring managers across French-speaking Switzerland who arrive from LinkedIn and need to understand Mickaël's Data Engineering profile quickly. Secondary: prospective clients and collaborators in French-speaking Switzerland.

## Product Purpose

Present Mickaël as a credible Data Engineer through a concise, durable GitHub profile. The hero should communicate the full data lifecycle without depending on projects, vendors, or technologies that will change over time.

## Brand Personality

Precise, credible, forward-looking.

## Anti-references

Avoid technology-logo collages, project names, generic terminal aesthetics, excessive neon, tiny text, visual clutter, and decorative motion that competes with the message.

## Design Principles

1. Explain the data-engineering value chain at a glance.
2. Keep the hero evergreen and independent from individual projects or tools.
3. Prioritize recruiter readability before technical spectacle.
4. Use motion to explain flow, not to decorate the page.
5. Verify every asset at its actual rendered size.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA contrast, preserve meaning without color, provide a complete static SVG for reduced-motion users, and ensure no label is clipped at desktop or mobile widths.
